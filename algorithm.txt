UNMISSABLE — THE RATING ALGORITHM, IN DETAIL
=============================================

Principle: the rating only measures what happened on the court, never who was playing.
A thrilling Hornets–Jazz must rank above a Lakers–Celtics decided at halftime.

Every number below lives in src/config/scoring.ts; the logic is in src/lib/scoring.ts.


STEP 1 — COMPETITIVE OR BLOWOUT?
--------------------------------

A game is COMPETITIVE if at least one of these is true:

  - margin at the end of regulation (after Q4, before any overtime) ........ <= 10
  - margin at the end of Q3 (the 4th quarter was still up for grabs) ........ <= 5
  - biggest lead of the team that LOST (a real comeback happened) .......... >= 12

Otherwise it's a BLOWOUT.

  competitive  ->  rating = 50 + C + P          (uncapped)
  blowout      ->  rating = min(49, C + P)      (a blowout stays a blowout)

  C = competitiveness and entertainment points (0 to 30)
  P = performance points (0 to infinity)


STEP 2a — COMPETITIVENESS AND ENTERTAINMENT (C, max 30)
-------------------------------------------------------

Each criterion becomes a value between 0 and 1, then is multiplied by its weight.
Values are clamped: past the "zero" threshold -> 0, past the "full" threshold -> 1,
linear in between.

  Criterion   Weight   Measured on                        0 at   1 at
  ---------   ------   --------------------------------   ----   ----
  Suspense      12     margin at the end of regulation      15      3
  Scenario       6     lead changes + times tied             0     20
  Comeback       6     biggest lead of the losing team       0     15
  Offense        6     total points per 48 minutes         210    260

  suspense = clamp((15 - regulationMargin) / (15 - 3))
  scenario = clamp((leadChanges + timesTied) / 20)
  comeback = clamp(loserBiggestLead / 15)
  offense  = clamp((pointsPer48 - 210) / (260 - 210))

  pointsPer48 = (homeScore + awayScore) x 48 / (48 + 5 x overtimes)

  C = 12 x suspense + 6 x scenario + 6 x comeback + 6 x offense

Notes:
  - Overtime has no bonus of its own. An overtime means a tie at the end of
    regulation, i.e. a regulation margin of 0, so it already gets full suspense.
    A one-point regulation game gets almost the same, so a high rating doesn't
    give away an overtime.
  - Points are normalized to 48 minutes, otherwise an overtime would inflate
    the total and reward the game twice.


STEP 2b — INDIVIDUAL PERFORMANCES (P, uncapped)
-----------------------------------------------

Each player's efficiency (the standard NBA "EFF"):

  EFF = PTS + REB + AST + STL + BLK - (FGA - FGM) - (FTA - FTM) - TOV

Every player above 30 adds points, on a curve that accelerates:

  P = sum over all players of  max(0, EFF - 30)^2 / 20

  Player EFF   Points
  ----------   ------
      30         0
      35         1.25
      40         5
      50        20
      60        45

Good games barely count, exceptional ones count a lot, and a 60 alone can push
a competitive game to 100. Every player counts, on both teams, so a duel of two
stars is rewarded naturally.


STEP 3 — RANKING AND DISPLAY
----------------------------

  - Games are ranked on the RAW rating, which can exceed 100
    (two games both shown at "100" keep their order).
  - The displayed rating is min(100, round(raw)).
  - The public JSON only contains the teams and the displayed rating,
    already sorted. Never the score.

SAN ANTONIO @ DENVER, 136-134 OT
--------------------------------
Competitive: tied 124-124 at the end of regulation.

  Suspense    regulation margin 0               clamp((15 - 0) / 12) = 1       12.00
  Scenario    6 lead changes + 4 ties = 10      10 / 20 = 0.5                    3.00
  Comeback    Spurs (lost) led by 13            13 / 15 = 0.867                  5.20
  Offense     270 pts in 53 min = 244.5 / 48    (244.5 - 210) / 50 = 0.69        4.14
                                                                           C = 24.34

  Wembanyama  34 pts, 18 reb, 7 ast, 1 stl, 5 blk,
              8/17 FG, 16/17 FT, 1 TOV -> EFF 54       (54 - 30)^2 / 20        28.80
  Jokic       40 pts, 8 reb, 13 ast, 0 stl, 3 blk,
              13/25 FG, 13/15 FT, 0 TOV -> EFF 50      (50 - 30)^2 / 20        20.00

  Rating = 50 + 24.34 + 48.80 = 123.14  ->  shown 100

TUNING
======

All thresholds are first guesses, to be calibrated on real nights — especially
the scenario ceiling (20) and the efficiency threshold (30). Change them in
src/config/scoring.ts; nothing else needs to move.
