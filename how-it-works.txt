How the rating works

After every game, Unmissable asks one question: was this game worth watching? It never looks at who was playing, only at what happened on the court.

First, was it a real game?
A game counts as competitive if it was close at the end of regulation, if the finish was tight, or if a team came back from a big deficit. Competitive games are rated from 50 up. A game that was close for three quarters, then decided in the fourth, starts a little lower. Blowouts never go above 49. A blowout stays a blowout, even if someone scores 60.

Then, how good was it?
The rating goes up with:
- suspense: the time spent within one or two possessions in the last minutes (and in overtime);
- twists: lead changes and ties, and the later they come, the more they count;
- comebacks: how big a lead the losing team let slip;
- offense: how many points were scored;
- big performances: every monster stat line adds to the rating, and the bigger it is, the more it counts. Two stars going at it is the best show there is.

The best game of the night sits on top. You'll know what to watch. You'll never know who won.
