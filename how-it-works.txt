How the rating works

Every morning, Unmissable looks at last night's games and asks one question: was this game worth watching? It never looks at who was playing — only at what happened on the court.

First, was it a real game?
A game counts as competitive if it was close at the end of regulation, if the fourth quarter was still up for grabs, or if a team came back from a big deficit. Competitive games are rated from 50 up; blowouts never go above 49. A blowout stays a blowout, even if someone scores 60.

Then, how good was it?
Among competitive games, the rating goes up with:
- suspense: how close it was at the end;
- twists: lead changes and ties;
- comebacks: how big a lead the losing team let slip;
- offense: how many points were scored;
- big performances: every monster stat line adds to the rating, and the bigger it is, the more it counts. Two stars going at it is the best show there is.

The best game of the night sits on top. You'll know what to watch. You'll never know who won.
